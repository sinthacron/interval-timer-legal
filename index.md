# Privacy Policy — Interval Timer

**Last updated: October 26, 2026**

## 1. Overview

Interval Timer is a free Android application developed and published by Sinthacron.

The app provides customizable timers for activities such as meditation, breathing exercises, relaxation, and interval training.

Interval Timer operates locally on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

## 2. Data Collection

Interval Timer does not require user registration or an account.

The app does not collect personal information, location data, health data, device identifiers, or usage analytics.

It does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services.

## 3. Local Data Storage

Interval Timer stores configuration and preferences locally on your device. This includes timer durations, intervals, cycle settings, sound preferences, and display settings.

This information is used solely to maintain your chosen app configuration.

The developer does not have access to these settings.

## 4. Android System Backups

Depending on your Android device and its settings, locally stored app preferences may be included in backups or device-to-device transfers managed by Android and your chosen backup provider.

These backup operations are controlled by the operating system and the relevant backup service, not by Interval Timer or its developer.

## 5. Notifications and Background Operation

Interval Timer uses Android notifications, vibration, audio playback, and background execution to provide timer functionality, including while the screen is off.

These features operate locally and do not transmit timer information to the developer.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Timer preferences remain stored locally until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Copies retained in Android system backups may remain subject to your device's and backup provider's retention and deletion policies.

Because Interval Timer does not maintain user accounts or store user information on developer-operated servers, there is no server-side account data to delete.
